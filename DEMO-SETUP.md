# Binance Demo Spot execution

V1 allows a small set of Spot reads and two narrowly constructed write requests on `https://demo-api.binance.com`. The test order request validates credentials and filters without sending an order to the matching engine. Real-exchange hosts and wallet, transfer, withdrawal, leverage, margin and futures paths are prohibited by the server adapter. Exchange and provider credentials stay in server-side Vercel environment variables.

## Enable Demo execution

Fresh production state starts stopped and Preview always blocks filled orders. With production `DEMO_EXECUTION_ENABLED` and `DEMO_AUTONOMOUS_ENABLED` set, scheduled cycles honor an already running state and renew signed Demo verification after the OpenAI completion benchmark passes. An administrator can still use **Verify Demo order access** and **Resume Demo execution** after a pause. Verification is bound to the source commit and Demo credentials and expires after seven days. **Emergency stop** prevents new dispatch authorizations immediately upon the GitHub write. An order already authorized for dispatch may still be in flight and must be reconciled.

The General Manager chooses and journals BUY, SELL, or HOLD. The Risk Manager independently verifies each order, applies exposure and exchange filters, and reconciles fills. New entries require current advisory evidence and a qualified neural score of at least 72% for the exact symbol and entry rule. The authorized fixed playbook activates qualified, expiring per-rule evidence automatically when autonomy is enabled. Ad hoc strategy revisions stay behind administrator approval.

The manager's separate Demo trial policy uses at most 1.5 USDT of full-principal risk, subject to saved risk and all existing limits. It allows one entry attempt per Gulf Standard Time day on a low-minimum pair and one open trial. A signed order-test preflight precedes each entry. The first exercise may validate the exchange lifecycle without a qualifying investment signal. The trial uses timed, gain, loss, and strategy exit conditions; execution is evaluated when a scheduled or manual cycle runs. Filled prices, estimated fee conversion, dust, realized results, and rejected orders feed the learning journal. Losing pair/rule combinations and rejected pairs enter a temporary cooldown. The model score is historical evidence and can be wrong. Exits for tracked positions retain their deterministic rules and can be evaluated when the AI advisory service is unavailable. Every entry reserves the entire principal as potential loss plus a fee buffer. This accounts for gaps, slippage and unavailable exits. The production allocation remains $100 with a 3% maximum per entry and the existing 2% daily loss cap. The app checks the live exchange minimum; some 1 USDT pairs can fit the limits. PAXG and XAUT are tokenized gold, with observed 5 USDT minimums that exceed available risk at this allocation. Gold remains eligible for research and shadow tests. Up to 10 daily dispatches count across buys and sells, with room reserved for exits. Shadow results are next-open-to-one-hour-open return measurements from Demo candles with an estimated fee, distinct from actual exchange orders. USDT is treated as an approximate USD reporting unit. Filled entry and exit volume both count toward daily volume, and the entry cap permits an exposure-reducing exit even when the daily volume is already at its limit.

## Reconciliation

Order dispatch uses a recorded client order ID. An uncertain response is reconciled with the authenticated order and fill records. A repeated cycle never resends a pending order. Unknown orders, incomplete fills and fees paid in unsupported assets leave a pending reservation and block additional orders. Investigate the order ID in the Demo account and run research again to retry reconciliation. If the exchange cannot confirm the outcome, keep execution stopped and reconcile the journal with the exchange before further trades.

Platform history records decisions, risk evaluations, strategy versions, prices, quantities, fees and realized results. The account-history view also shows recent outside orders with separate attribution and indicates incomplete fee coverage. Outside orders do not contribute to the platform portfolio or learning performance. The application schedules no intrabar monitoring or protective orders. Exits are evaluated in research cycles. Exchange quantity filters can leave dust that requires operator review.
